# Skarbiec domain model & invariants

Target model. Items marked *(target — spec-0x)* are not implemented yet; do not assume they exist in code, and do not build around their absence either — check `skarbiec-plan/domain.md` and the owning spec first.

## Assets and transactions

- `AssetClass` (`Skarbiec.Contracts`): Cash = 0, Deposit = 1, Stock = 2, Etf = 3, Bond = 4, PreciousMetal = 6, BankAccount = 9 (explicit values — the ints are stored, so never renumber; removed members leave gaps).
- `TransactionType`: Buy, Sell, Deposit, Withdraw, Dividend, Interest. **Buy/Deposit increase quantity, Sell/Withdraw decrease it**; Dividend and Interest do not change quantity. Quantity is derived from transactions, never stored as an independent truth (ADR-009).

## Valuation modes

`ValuationMode` is explicit on the asset, defaulted per class by `AssetValuationModes` in `Skarbiec.Contracts`:

| Mode | Default for | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Market` | Stock, Etf, PreciousMetal | `Quantity × QuoteUnitsPerQuantity × last PriceQuote(instrument, ≤ date) × FxRate(quoteCurrency→PLN, ≤ date)` — the multiplier is 1 except for PreciousMetal (fine grams per piece) |
| `CurrencyValued` | Cash, Deposit, BankAccount, Bond | `Quantity × FxRate(assetCurrency→PLN, ≤ date)` — no instrument |

The default is a starting point the user may override; the mode, not the class, decides how a value is computed. An asset has exactly one mode, and `Market` requires an `InstrumentId`.

## Valuation algorithm

Use the **last known** price and FX rate at or before the snapshot date (weekends, holidays, gaps). Mark the line `IsStale` when the price or rate used is more than 7 days older than the snapshot date. Missing data never zeroes a position — it produces a stale line.

## Currencies

- The user-chosen currency (`Asset.Currency`) comes from `SupportedCurrencies` in `Skarbiec.Contracts` — PLN, EUR, USD; default PLN; matched uppercase and exactly. Frontend mirror: `web/src/app/shared/currencies.ts`.
- MarketData keeps its **own** currency catalog: `Currency (Code PK, Name, Symbol, DecimalPlaces, DisplayOrder)`, seeded PLN, EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, driving `FxSyncJob`. It is deliberately wider than `SupportedCurrencies` — `Instrument.QuoteCurrency` and `FxRate.Pair` (e.g. `USDPLN`) are not restricted to the user-facing set. PLN is the fixed base (ADR-008); there is no pivot flag and no hardcoded fallback rate — a missing history is backfilled by the job.

## Entities per service

**Identity** — `User` (`DisplayName`, no `BaseCurrency` — PLN-only, ADR-008), `RefreshToken`.

**Portfolio** — `Account (Id, UserId, Name, Type, Wrapper, Currency, IsArchived)`, unique `(UserId, Name)`, `Type` Brokerage | Bonds | Bank and `Wrapper` Regular | Ike | Ikze stored as strings and fixed at creation, `Currency` fixed too and PLN except on a Bank account, which is always Regular; `BankAccountTerms (AccountId PK+FK, UserId, Variant, BankName?, AnnualInterestRatePercent)`, the terms of a Bank account, 1:1 with it, `Variant` Current | Savings stored as a string — its balance is its money asset's `Quantity`; `CashPlace (Id, UserId, Name, IsArchived)`, unique `(UserId, Name)`, a physical cash place holding one Cash balance asset per currency; `Asset (Id, UserId, AccountId?, CashPlaceId?, AssetClass, ValuationMode, Name, Currency, Quantity, InstrumentId?, Version, IsArchived, xmin)`; `Transaction (Id, UserId, AssetId, Type, Quantity, UnitPriceAmount, FxRateToPln?, AmountPln?, FeeAmount?, Date, xmin)`, where `FxRateToPln` is the `{Asset.Currency}PLN` rate frozen at write time — the latest MarketData rate on or before `Date`, `1` for PLN, `null` when there is none that early (ADR-026), except a stock/ETF Buy/Sell (no rate is 400 `Validation.FxRateMissing`) and a stock/ETF Dividend/Interest (always `1`, its amount is PLN), `AmountPln` is the PLN really paid or received (without the fee) on a non-PLN stock/ETF Buy/Sell — it then sets `FxRateToPln = AmountPln / (Quantity × UnitPriceAmount)` and the cash leg, any other use is 400 `Validation.PlnAmountNotAllowed` — and `FeeAmount` is the PLN broker fee, only on a stock/ETF Buy/Sell; `TermDeposit (AssetId PK+FK, UserId, BankName?, Principal, StartDate, TermLength, TermUnit, MaturityDate, AnnualInterestRatePercent, Capitalization, EarlyBreakInterestLossPercent, SourceAccountId, ReturnAccountId, SettledOn?, SettledGrossInterest?, SettledTax?, RolloverCount)`, the terms of a Deposit-class asset (term-deposits), 1:1 with it — `SourceAccountId` and `ReturnAccountId` are plain ids of Bank accounts, no FK; its projection and Active/Due/Settled/PaidOut status are computed at read time by `DepositInterestMath` (PaidOut: settled and holding a payout `Withdraw`; the tax is always `BelkaTax`); the `Settled*` fields are what the bank actually paid, set by `SettleDeposit` once per term; `RollOverDeposit` (deposit-rollover) starts the next term on the same asset — the whole balance as principal, the old maturity date as start date, only the rate changed — clearing them and incrementing the stored `RolloverCount`; `MetalHolding (AssetId PK+FK, UserId, Metal, FineWeightGramsPerPiece)`, the product of a PreciousMetal-class asset (metal-holdings), 1:1 with it — `Asset.Quantity` is in pieces, the instrument is fixed per metal by `MetalInstruments`, and `FineWeightGramsPerPiece` travels as `AssetPositionChanged.QuoteUnitsPerQuantity`; `TreasuryBond (AssetId PK+FK, UserId, SeriesCode, Type, PurchaseDate, BondCount, PurchasePricePerBond, FirstPeriodRatePercent, MarginPercent?, EarlyRedemptionFeePerBond, MaturityDate, SwappedFromAssetId?)`, the terms of a Bond-class asset (bond-purchase) — one purchase lot of a retail treasury bond, PLN, 1:1 with it — whose maturity and interest periods come from the pure `BondSchedule` and whose Active/InterestDue/Matured/Redeemed status is computed at read time, `SwappedFromAssetId` the matured bond a swap bought it with (no FK, cleared when that bond is removed); `BondRedemption (Id, UserId, AssetId, Kind, Date, BondCount, CapitalisedInterest, DiscountIncome, AccruedInterest, Fee, Tax, Proceeds, CreditTransactionId?, ChargeTransactionId?, CashTransferId?, SwapTargetAssetId?, SwapTransferId?)`, a holding redeemed whole at maturity (bond-maturity-swap, kind `Maturity` or `Swap`, `taxable = k × (c + δ)`, `proceeds = k × (100 + c) − tax`) or k of its bonds before it (bond-early-redemption, kind `Early`, `taxable = max(0, k × (i − f + δ))`, `proceeds = k × (100 + i − f) − tax`, the fee capped at the accrued interest except from period 2 of a coupon type), amounts from the pure `BondRedemptionMath`, indexed on `AssetId` (not unique), deleted with its asset in the handler; `BankInterestSettlement (Id, UserId, AssetId, PeriodStart, PeriodEnd, GrossInterest, Tax, TransactionId?)`, one settled calendar month of a Bank account's interest, keyed by the account's money asset, unique `(AssetId, PeriodEnd)`, fixed once stored, `TransactionId` the system-managed net `Deposit` credit dated `PeriodEnd` (null when the net is 0); `BondInterestSettlement (Id, UserId, AssetId, PeriodIndex, PeriodStart, PeriodEnd, RatePercent, BondCount, GrossInterest, Tax, CreditTransactionId?, TransferId?)`, one settled interest period of a treasury bond (bond-interest-periods), unique `(AssetId, PeriodIndex)`, fixed once stored, `CreditTransactionId` the system-managed `Deposit` on the bond dated `PeriodEnd` (a coupon's net, moved out by the Bond → Cash transfer `TransferId`; a capitalising type's gross), deleted with its asset in the handler; `BondInterestMath` computes the per-bond gross (OTS by day count over 365, ROR/DOR a twelfth of the rate, COI the rate, TOS/EDO/ROS/ROD `C_k − C_{k−1}` over the compounded, unrounded rates), tax by `BelkaTax` for coupons only, skipped when the bond's account wrapper is not Regular. Bank interest periods are calendar months from the earliest transaction's date, Due from the day after they end (Europe/Warsaw); `BankInterestMath` accrues gross = `round(Σ end-of-day balance × rate / 100 / 365, 2)` once per period with the account's current rate for every day (earlier credits included, so interest compounds monthly), tax always by the shared `BelkaTax` rule, and skips zero-interest months. Deletes cascade explicitly in the handler, not the database (no FKs inside `portfolio_db` except `TermDeposit → Asset`, `BankAccountTerms → Account`, `TreasuryBond → Asset` and `MetalHolding → Asset`, which also cascade): deleting an asset removes its transactions — every removal and outbox event in one `SaveChangesAsync`. No denormalized child counters. `Asset.Version` is the per-asset ordering counter on `AssetPositionChanged`, written only by `PositionEventPublisher`; `xmin` stays the concurrency token and is a separate thing (it doesn't move when only the archived flag changes).

**Portfolio watchlist** — `WatchlistItem (Id, UserId, InstrumentId, AssetClass, Ticker, Name, Exchange?, QuoteCurrency, Note?, TargetPrice?, AddedAtUtc)`, unique `(UserId, InstrumentId)`, Stock or Etf only (400 `Validation.UnsupportedAssetClass`; a repeat add is 409 `Conflict.InstrumentAlreadyWatched`); the instrument fields are a snapshot from the add-time lookup, prices and holdings are read live, add/remove publish `WatchlistItemAdded`/`WatchlistItemRemoved`, update publishes nothing (ADR-035).

**MarketData** — `Instrument (Id, Ticker, Name, Source, QuoteCurrency, AssetClass, VerificationStatus, HistoryCoveredFrom?)`, where `InstrumentVerificationStatus` is `Verified | Unverified | Failed` and records the ADR-018 ticker check made once at creation; it is never re-checked from a valuation path, and `Verified` must stay the 0 member (it is the column's `HasDefaultValue`). `PriceQuote`; `FxRate`; `SyncRun (+ Kind: Prices | Fx | Backfill | BondCatalog)`; `Currency`; `BondSeries (Code PK, Type, Isin, SaleStart, SaleEnd, IssuePrice, SwapPrice?, MarginPercent?, UpdatedAtUtc)` + `BondSeriesPeriodRate ((SeriesCode, PeriodIndex) PK, RatePercent)`, the global MF retail bond catalog kept by `BondCatalogSyncJob`; `InstrumentUsage (InstrumentId PK, AssetCount, FirstUsedAt)`, derived from per-asset `AssetInstrumentLink (AssetId PK, InstrumentId?, FirstTransactionDate?, Version, IsRemoved)` rows maintained from `AssetPositionChanged`/`AssetRemoved` so `PriceSyncJob` syncs only instruments actually in use; a live holding whose first transaction predates `HistoryCoveredFrom` triggers a history backfill from that date.

**Reporting** — `Position`, a local read model upserted from `AssetPositionChanged` (the event payload plus `UpdatedAt`, `AssetId` as the primary key, `AccountId?` copied from the event; valued unless the asset's own `IsArchived` is set); `AssetValuation (Id, UserId, AccountId?, AssetId, Date, AssetClass, Quantity, PriceUsed?, PriceDate?, FxRateUsed?, ValuePln, IsStale)`, one line per asset per date and the basis for P/L, goals and TWR; `ValuationSnapshot (Id, UserId, Date, TotalPln, IsStale)`, one per user per day (ADR-033) — the per-class breakdown is a `GROUP BY AssetClass` over the lines, not a stored JSON blob. `TargetAllocation (UserId PK, UpdatedAt, Groups)`, the user's one target across all accounts — `Groups` a JSON complex collection of `(Name, AssetClasses, TargetPercent, BandPp)` in the saved order, saved whole by `PUT /api/reporting/target-allocation`, compared with the latest valuation by the pure `TargetAllocationMath`. `GroupTargetAllocation (UserId + GroupId PK, UpdatedAt, Groups)`, the same for one asset group (`PUT/GET/DELETE .../target-allocation/groups/{groupId}`, compared with that group's lines only, publishes nothing, removed by `AssetGroupDeleted`, ADR-041). Insights configuration *(target)*: `EmergencyFund` + `EmergencyFundAsset`, `SavingsGoal` + `SavingsGoalAsset`. Insight math (`AllocationMath`, `RebalancingMath`, `ContributionPlanMath`, `EmergencyFundMath`, `GoalMath`) is pure functions over `AssetValuation` — nothing is recomputed from other services on request.

## Invariants (validate and test)

- Target-allocation group targets sum to exactly 100 (one decimal each, a class in at most one group); each group carries its own ± pp band, and the base for shares is the grouped classes only.
- A Sell or Withdraw may never take an asset's quantity below 0 — **at any point in its transaction history**, not just at the end (editing or deleting an older transaction must be re-checked against the whole timeline).
- Amounts and quantities are ≥ 0.
- An asset's currency is immutable once it has transactions — each transaction's frozen PLN rate belongs to that currency (ADR-026).
- A Cash/Deposit/BankAccount/Bond asset's transactions are only Deposit/Withdraw and a Stock/Etf's only Buy/Sell/Dividend/Interest — 400 `Validation.TransactionTypeNotAllowed` on every write (record, edit, opening transaction, class change); the rule lives in Portfolio's `AssetTransactionTypes`, mirrored by `allowedTransactionTypes` in the web client.
- A Stock/Etf Buy/Sell writes a trade pair with the security's account cash (a Buy a Withdraw of `round(q × p × r, 2) + fee`, a Sell a Deposit of `round(q × p × r, 2) − fee` (`AmountPln` replacing the rounded product when set), `r` its frozen rate, PLN, unit price 1, FX 1), a Dividend/Interest a Deposit of its net PLN amount; a `cashAssetId` on a Stock/Etf is 400 `Validation.CashLinkNotAllowed`, a `feeAmount` outside a stock/ETF Buy/Sell 400 `Validation.FeeNotAllowed`, a fee above a Sell's gross 400 `Validation.FeeExceedsProceeds`. Update and delete work from the security leg; a negative account cash on any day is 400 `Validation.InsufficientFunds`.
- A deposit is opened only from the user's unarchived Bank account (`sourceAccountId`; 400 `Validation.BankAccountRequired` for another type), takes its currency, and the opening Deposit is the In leg of a BankAccount → Deposit transfer (a negative balance on any day is 400 `Validation.InsufficientFunds`); `returnAccountId?` defaults to the source and must be an unarchived Bank account in that currency (400 `Validation.BankAccountRequired` / `Validation.TransferCurrencyMismatch`), editable until the payout. A Bank account that is the return account of a deposit not yet paid out cannot be archived or deleted (409 `Conflict.AccountHasOpenDeposit`).
- A Deposit-class asset has exactly one `TermDeposit`; its transactions are system-managed (one opening Deposit, rewritten by `UpdateDeposit` only before the first rollover, plus the net-interest Deposit each settled term adds when the net is above 0, and the payout's Withdraw leg) — record/update/delete on it is 409 `Conflict.DepositTransactionsManaged`, and `AddAsset`/`UpdateAsset` reject class Deposit or a change to/from it with 400 `Validation.UseDepositEndpoints`.
- A Bond asset has exactly one `TreasuryBond`; its transactions are system-managed (one opening Deposit of `bondCount × purchasePricePerBond`, optionally the In leg of a Cash → Bond transfer, rewritten in place by `UpdateBond`; plus one interest credit per settled period and, for a coupon, the Out leg of its Bond → Cash transfer; at redemption a discount credit, a tax charge and the Out legs (early: a credit of the accrued interest less the capitalised plus the discount, a charge of the fee plus the tax) paying the proceeds Bond → Cash and, in a swap, Bond → Bond into the new bond's opening Deposit) — record/update/delete on it is 409 `Conflict.BondTransactionsManaged`, and `AddAsset`/`UpdateAsset` reject class Bond or a change to/from it with 400 `Validation.UseBondEndpoints`.
- A BankAccount asset is the money of a Bank account, created only with it — `AddAsset`/`UpdateAsset` reject class BankAccount with 400 `Validation.UseAccountEndpoints`; its transactions are ordinary Deposit/Withdraw.
- A PreciousMetal asset has exactly one `MetalHolding`, created and edited only through the metal slices (always Market, PLN, its metal's instrument); its transactions are only Buy/Sell, and a new metal holds 0 pieces (no first purchase) — `AddAsset`/`UpdateAsset` reject class PreciousMetal or a change to/from it with 400 `Validation.UseMetalEndpoints`.
- Bank interest periods settle in order, one per calendar month — only the next due period's end is accepted (409 `Conflict.BankInterestNotDue` / `Conflict.BankInterestPeriodMismatch`); later history edits never recompute a stored settlement. A rate change applies from the next unsettled period. The interest endpoints live under the account: a non-Bank account is 400 `Validation.InterestNotSupported`, an archived one 409 `Conflict.AccountArchived`.
- Only the latest bank interest settlement can be undone (409 `Conflict.BankSettlementNotLatest`); an undo whose credit a later Withdraw spent is 400 `Validation.InsufficientFunds`.
- Bond interest periods settle in order — only the next unsettled, ended periods are accepted (409 `Conflict.BondInterestPeriodMismatch` / `Conflict.BondInterestNotDue`); only the latest bond settlement can be undone (409 `Conflict.BondSettlementNotLatest`); a bond with any settlement keeps its terms (`UpdateBond` is 409 `Conflict.BondSettled`).
- An early redemption takes 1 to the held count (400 `Validation.BondRedemptionCount`), dated after purchase, before maturity and not after today (400 `Validation.BondEarlyRedemptionDate`), with every period ending by then settled (409 `Conflict.BondInterestUnsettled`) and never before the latest settled period's end or redemption (409 `Conflict.BondEarlyRedemptionOutOfOrder`); `TreasuryBond.BondCount` drops by k, at 0 the bond is `Redeemed`, and a bond with any redemption keeps its terms (`UpdateBond` is 409 `Conflict.BondRedeemed`).
- A bond is redeemed or swapped whole, once, from its maturity date with every period settled (409 `Conflict.BondNotMatured` / `Conflict.BondInterestUnsettled` / `Conflict.BondAlreadyRedeemed`), everything dated the maturity date; a redeemed bond holds 0, its settlements are fixed (409 `Conflict.BondRedeemed`), and a swap-born bond keeps its count, price, purchase date, series, type and tax exemption (409 `Conflict.BondFromSwap`).
- Bank interest credits are system-managed — update/delete through the transaction endpoints is 409 `Conflict.BankInterestManaged`.
- A settled recurring-income occurrence, or a settled one-off income (`OneOffIncome.TransactionId?`, locked against update and delete as a whole, 409 `Conflict.OneOffIncomeSettled`), may own a `Deposit` on its settlement account's money asset (`RecurringIncomeOccurrence.TransactionId?`, restrict FK; the settlement's frozen rate): settling with `createDeposit` creates it, every re-settle updates, moves or removes it, undo deletes it, and each save publishes `AssetPositionChanged` for every touched money asset; a negative replay on any day is 400 `Validation.InsufficientFunds`, a create/change/removal on an archived account 409 `Conflict.AccountArchived`, and update/delete through the transaction endpoints 409 `Conflict.IncomeDepositManaged`; `TransactionResponse.recurringIncomeName` carries the income's current name (a one-off income's too).
- A settled deposit's terms are immutable — `UpdateDeposit` on it is 409 `Conflict.DepositSettled`, except a request that changes only `returnAccountId` while it is not yet paid out; deleting it stays allowed.
- A rolled-over deposit's principal and start date are fixed — `UpdateDeposit` changing either is 409 `Conflict.DepositRolledOver`.
- A paid-out deposit holds 0; a payout always moves the whole balance to the return account, as a Deposit → BankAccount transfer.
- An account holds Stock/Etf (Brokerage) or Bond (Bonds) assets; a Bank account holds only its money; every other class sits in none. A missing account is 400 `Validation.AccountRequired`, the wrong type 400 `Validation.AccountTypeMismatch`, an account on another class (a Cash asset included) 400 `Validation.AccountNotAllowed`, and `UpdateAsset` never changes it. It is archived only with every asset, its cash included, at quantity 0 (409 `Conflict.AccountNotEmpty`) and deleted only when it holds no asset other than its cash and the cash has no transaction (409 `Conflict.AccountHasHistory`); neither cascades, and a delete publishes only the cash's `AssetRemoved`.
- Every account owns one money asset with `AccountId` set (`cashAssetId`, `cashBalance` on `AccountResponse`): class Cash in PLN for Brokerage and Bonds, class BankAccount in the account's currency for Bank. It is created in the account's save, renamed with it and hidden from `ListAssets` and the cash-place candidates. It is system-managed: `UpdateAsset`/`RemoveAsset`/`ArchiveAsset`/`RestoreAsset` on it are 409 `Conflict.AccountCashManaged`. Its money moves through ordinary Deposit/Withdraw transactions and the manual account-to-account transfer between two different unarchived accounts of any type in the same currency (400 `Validation.TransferCurrencyMismatch` otherwise; a stranger's source is 404); any write that makes its replay negative on any day is 400 `Validation.InsufficientFunds`, same-day inflows first. A Brokerage account's cash is published under the class it counts under each day (`AssetPositionChanged.ClassHistory`, ADR-039: its latest held security's class, else Cash; a Bonds account's is Bond), and Reporting values every line with that day's class (`Position.ClassOn`).
- A cash place (`CashPlace`, unique `(UserId, Name)`, 409 `Conflict.DuplicateName`) owns one hidden Cash balance per currency (`Asset.CashPlaceId` set, `AccountId` null, unique `(CashPlaceId, Currency)`; 409 `Conflict.DuplicateCurrency`), created in the place's save and hidden from `ListAssets`; a Cash asset has exactly one of `AccountId` or `CashPlaceId`, and `AddAsset` rejects every Cash asset with 400 `Validation.UseCashPlaceEndpoints`. The balances are system-managed: `UpdateAsset`/`RemoveAsset`/`ArchiveAsset`/`RestoreAsset` on one are 409 `Conflict.CashPlaceManaged`. Money enters and leaves through ordinary Deposit/Withdraw transactions and the manual Bank account's money ↔ place balance transfer (ATM withdrawal, bank deposit; same currency, else 400 `Validation.TransferCurrencyMismatch`; place → place or a place ↔ a Brokerage/Bonds account's cash is 400 `Validation.InvalidTransferCounterpart`); a write that makes a balance's replay negative on any day is 400 `Validation.InsufficientFunds`. A place archives only with every balance at 0 (409 `Conflict.CashPlaceNotEmpty`) and is read-only while archived (409 `Conflict.CashPlaceArchived`, checked after the tenancy lookup), deletes only with no transaction on any balance (409 `Conflict.CashPlaceHasHistory`) and publishes `AssetRemoved` per balance. `ListCashPlaces`/`GetCashPlace` value the balances at the latest `{CUR}PLN` rate from `POST /internal/fx/latest-batch`, failing soft (`fxUnavailable`, null foreign values, ADR-036). A precious-metal Buy/Sell settles through a required `cashAssetId`, a place's PLN balance or a PLN Bank account's money (400 `Validation.SettlementSourceRequired` without it; another currency 400 `Validation.TransferCurrencyMismatch`, anything else 400 `Validation.InvalidTransferCounterpart`), and takes the invoice `totalAmount` instead of `unitPrice` (400 `Validation.MetalTotalRequired`): the cash leg is exactly the total and the stored unit price `round(totalAmount / quantity, 2)`.
- An IKE or IKZE account may hold an `AccountContributionLimit` per calendar year (`PUT/DELETE .../contribution-limits/{year}`, amount > 0, year 2000-2100: 400 `Validation.ContributionLimit`; a Regular account 400 `Validation.ContributionLimitNotAllowed`); `AccountResponse.contribution` is the year's external Deposits plus incoming transfers from another account's cash, null for Regular, and the limit is display only.
- An archived account is read-only: every asset and transaction write in it is 409 `Conflict.AccountArchived`, checked after the tenancy lookup so a stranger still gets 404. Renaming or deleting the account stays allowed.
- An archived asset (asset-archive, `Asset.IsArchived`, independent of its account's flag) is read-only the same way: every asset, transaction and deposit write on it is 409 `Conflict.AssetArchived`, checked after the account check through `ArchivedAccountGuard`; removal stays allowed, and it is never a transfer candidate or counterpart. It drops out of net worth from the archive date; earlier snapshots stay.
- Unique: one `PriceQuote` per (instrument, date), one `FxRate` per (pair, date), one `AssetValuation` per (asset, date).
- Every user-owned entity has `UserId` — enforced by a NetArchTest architecture test.
- Cross-service references are plain `Guid` columns, no FK (`Asset.InstrumentId` → MarketData; `Position.AssetId` → Portfolio).

## Conscious simplifications

Transactions are single-entry: a Dividend or Interest on one asset creates no matching cash inflow anywhere. Transfers are the exception — their two legs (a Withdraw and a Deposit, or a Cash Withdraw/Deposit and a precious metal's Buy/Sell, or an account cash's Withdraw/Deposit and a stock's or ETF's Buy/Sell/Dividend/Interest, with the money amount on the cash leg) are linked by `TransferId` and changed only by their entry point (asset-transfers-deposit-funding, metal-cash-legs, accounts-brokerage-trades; a stock's or ETF's pair is changed and deleted from the security leg). A transaction's PLN value is its `AmountPln` when entered, else frozen at the transaction-date rate — never recomputed later; a `null` rate fills in only when that transaction is edited (ADR-026). The base currency is PLN only — the user picks a currency per asset, but every valuation is reported in PLN.
